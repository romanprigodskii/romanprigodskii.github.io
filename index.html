Vertex Boxing

Concluded research, September 2026. The report and the code are public.

It began from a thesis:

Regional and club boxing lines are soft enough to beat.

On every bout the available odds cover, the data says the reverse: the model gains most on the market at the top of the sport, and least on club cards.

On its own the model forecasts worse than the opening price. Blended in, it moves that price, posted a median of three days before the fight, 87% of the way to where it closes.

A live test was frozen and published on 24 September 2026, before any bout it covers had been fought. It closes on 25 September 2029. Every result so far is a backtest; no real bet has been placed.

Under review

E-Values: From Statistics to ML, a NeurIPS 2026 workshop, and an extended version at NewInML. Notification on 29 September 2026.

PDFExtended version

Testing by betting when the bets are real: an e-value audit of 84 pre-registered market-efficiency hypotheses

E-values test a hypothesis by betting against it. Here the other side of the bet is a real bookmaker: 84 pre-registered ideas about where Vertex MMA might beat the market, each re-tested as a bet at the posted prices, over 1,787 fights. The headline result is negative, and the paper says so.

Working paper

PDF

Measure the instrument first: detection floors for model selection and for LLM judges

How small an improvement can a model evaluation actually see? Retraining the same model under a different random seed changes nothing real, yet it reproduces 80% of the only improvement Vertex MMA’s winner model ever shipped.
