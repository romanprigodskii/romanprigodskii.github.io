Accepted

Testing by betting when the bets are real: an e-value audit of 84 pre-registered hypotheses about a deployed forecaster2026

Sole author. Accepted as a poster at two NeurIPS 2026 workshops, both non-archival: New in ML, and in a shorter version, E-Values: From Statistics to ML.

Sole author. Poster at two NeurIPS 2026 workshops (non-archival): New in ML; E-Values: From Statistics to ML.

  • Real bets: a deployed UFC forecaster against a bookmaker’s posted prices, over 4,141 walk-forward bouts.
  • The 84 slices overlap, so Benjamini–Hochberg’s guarantee is not established; its four discoveries survive neither Benjamini–Yekutieli nor e-BH.
  • Checked on 40,000 null replications (expected e-value 0.985 ± 0.018). The headline result is negative.

Working paper

Measure the instrument first: detection floors for model selection and for LLM judges2026

Sole author. Measures the smallest effect an evaluation could have detected, with a null lever: the same recipe refit under a new random seed, which changes nothing real.

Sole author. Measures the smallest effect an evaluation could have detected, by refitting under a new seed.

  • Re-seeding alone reproduces 80% of the only change Vertex MMA’s winner model ever shipped; the pipeline had 56% power against its own result.
  • Identical reruns of a deployed LLM classifier disagree on 5.6% of items: a floor of 2.9 agreement points.

Concluded

Vertex Boxing: the Vertex MMA models moved to professional boxingJul–Sep 2026

Built on 413,279 professional bouts to test whether regional and club betting lines are soft enough to beat; where prices exist, they are not. Blended into the opening price, the model passed a test registered in public before its holdout model was trained, at e = 41.8 against a bar of 20 (January to July 2026). Backtests only, no real bets; a live test frozen in public on 24 September 2026 runs to 2029.

Tested on 413,279 bouts whether regional and club boxing lines are soft: where priced, they are not. Blended into the opening price, it passed a pre-registered backtest at e = 41.8 against a bar of 20 (2026 holdout).
